\textbf{Re: Grokking the Discrete Logarithm: Scaling Across Model Capacity and Mechanistic Analysis}

Dear Editors-in-Chief,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Grokking the Discrete Logarithm: Scaling Across Model Capacity and Mechanistic Analysis,'' for publication in the \emph{Journal of Machine Learning Research}.

The paper studies the discrete logarithm problem as a controlled machine-learning benchmark for delayed algorithmic generalization. To the best of my knowledge, it provides the first systematic demonstration of grokking on the full variable-base DLP mapping, together with a capacity sweep from 90k to 14.4M parameters, mechanistic spectral analysis, symbolic regression of hidden representations, prime-order controls, and generalization to generators excluded from training.
